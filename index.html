Install in about 2 minutes

  1. Download the file above and unzip it — you'll get a trader-workspace folder.
  2. Open your browser's extensions page: type chrome://extensions (Chrome) or edge://extensions (Edge) in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (the toggle on that page).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the trader-workspace folder.
  5. Click the VigyaanVest icon in your toolbar to open the workspace.
How to use it
Add & find stocks
  • Press Ctrl+K (Mac: ⌘+K) or click + Add to open the search.
  • Type a ticker and press Enter to open it. Type one that isn't on your list to add it.
  • For India, add the suffix: TCS.NS (NSE) or RELIANCE.BO (BSE). No suffix = US.
  • Use the Filter symbols… box in the watchlist to narrow the list by ticker, name, or tag.
Deck — research one stock
  • Click a symbol in the watchlist to load live price, fundamentals, a chart, and your notes.
  • Everything in the right panels is editable and saved locally: change Status (dropdown), pick a Setup chip, and click the Bull / Bear / Trigger / Stop / Review text to type.
  • Add tags by typing in the tags box and pressing Enter.
  • Use Quick links to open Yahoo / Finviz / Screener / TradingView for that symbol.
Grid — screen your whole watchlist
  • Switch to Grid (top bar) to see every name as a sortable table.
  • Click a column header to sort (click again to reverse).
  • Use the left filters — Market, Status, Signal (event this week / in the tape / review overdue).
  • Click a row for the detail drawer, then Open in Deck to dive in.
The Tape — live news + read-aloud

The Tape only fills while a supported news site is open in a tab — that's how it reads headlines.

  • In the Deck's right rail, click a source under “Open a source to feed the tape” — TradingView, Finviz, MarketWatch, or Zerodha Pulse. Headlines start streaming in.
  • Click ▶ on the Squawk bar to read the top headlines aloud.
  • In Settings → The Tape: choose voice / rate / volume, and turn on “Remember what was read” so ▶ doesn't repeat (or Clear read history to start over).
Calendar — earnings & macro events
  • The strip at the bottom of Deck shows the next ~12 trading days. Click Open full calendar for Month / Week.
  • Move through time with ‹ ›, or click Today to jump back to the current month/week.
  • Click a ★ watchlist earnings entry to open that stock in Deck.
  • It syncs automatically twice a day; force it any time with Settings → Data & sync → Sync now.
Alerts — earnings & macro reminders

Alerts are automatic for the stocks on your watchlist — no per-stock setup.

  • Add a stock to your watchlist and its next earnings auto-arms a desktop reminder.
  • Set how far ahead and the time of day in Settings → Calendar & alerts (and toggle which US / India macro events alert you).
  • Allow notifications for your browser in your OS settings so reminders can show.
Dock — read the page you're on
  • While viewing a stock page (Yahoo Finance, Screener.in, NSE, SEC), open the Dock (panel icon, top-right) — it reads that page's key metrics into a side panel.
  • Click Open in full terminal to jump into the full Deck for that symbol.
Settings, themes & backup
  • Toggle Light / Midnight with the moon icon (top-right), or in Settings → Appearance (also default view & density).
  • Research options: show the TradingView chart embed, and read metrics from the open page (Dock).
  • Back up your watchlist, notes & settings: Settings → Data & sync → Export backup (JSON); restore with Import (merge).
Everything you enter stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
