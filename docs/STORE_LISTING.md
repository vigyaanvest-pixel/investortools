## Short description (≤132 chars)
Unified pro-trader workspace: Symbol 360 research, Market Calendar, and a live news Tape in one Deck/Grid terminal.

## Detailed description

> One connected workspace for active investors — research, calendar, and news in a single
> terminal, instead of three separate extensions.
>
> • **Deck** — a focused single-symbol terminal: watchlist rail, live quote + fundamentals
>   (P/E, ROE, ROCE, D/E, 52-week, market cap), a chart, and your own thesis / setup / tags
>   / review notes.
> • **Grid** — your watchlist as a sortable screener: live change, sparkline, next event
>   countdown, and "in the news" count, with filters and an expand-in-place detail drawer.
> • **The Tape** — a live headline stream pulled from TradingView News Flow, Finviz,
>   MarketWatch, and Zerodha Pulse, with optional read-aloud (Squawk).
> • **Calendar** — earnings + US Fed / India RBI macro events, a forward ribbon, a full
>   Month/Week overlay, and desktop alerts before the events you care about.
> • **Dock** — a side panel that reads metrics off the stock page you're viewing.
> • **⌘K** — jump to any symbol, or type a new ticker (AAPL, TCS.NS, RELIANCE.BO) to add it.
>
> Mixed **US ($)** and **India (₹)** markets. Light + Midnight themes. **Local-first** —
> your watchlist, notes, and settings stay in your browser; there is no account, no
> tracking, and no backend. Research/workflow tool only — not investment advice.
